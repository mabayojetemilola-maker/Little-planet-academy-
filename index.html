About Us

A Warm, Safe Place to Learn & Grow

Little Planet Academy is more than a school — it is a second home where children are loved, guided, and prepared for a bright future.

Nurturing the Whole Child

At Little Planet Academy we believe every child is a unique star. Our mission is to provide an affordable, high-quality education that develops academic ability, moral character, and social confidence.

Located in the heart of Giginyu, Kano, we combine a safe learning environment with dedicated teachers, interactive lessons, and strong Islamic values so that every pupil leaves ready for the next stage of life.

Academic & Sectional Offerings

Four Pathways, One Loving Community

We offer an integrated educational track designed for every stage of early childhood and primary learning.

👶

Crèche

Early childcare and infant supervision in a safe, warm, and nurturing environment where little ones feel secure and loved.

🎨

Nursery

Play-based learning focused on social growth, curiosity, and early cognitive development through fun, hands-on activities.

📘

Primary

Rigorous basic education emphasising literacy, numeracy, critical thinking, and general sciences to build a strong foundation.

🕌

Islamiyya

Dedicated Islamic religious and moral teachings thoughtfully integrated alongside standard academic learning.

Curriculum & Teaching Approach

Learning That Sticks — and Inspires

We blend the best of Nigerian and international frameworks with interactive methods and strong character formation.

01

Blended Framework

We combine elements of the Nigerian national curriculum with international early-years frameworks, giving children a broad and balanced learning baseline.

02

Interactive Methodology

Lessons are highly interactive — activity-driven modules, songs, games and hands-on exercises replace dry lectures so children learn by doing and enjoying.

03

Character Development

Alongside academics we actively emphasise moral values, community awareness, respect, and life-readiness skills that last a lifetime.
